A considered beginning

A gentleman is not a label.
It is a way of moving through the world.

GENTLEMAN 101™ brings together mentorship, reflection, and age-appropriate resources to support the habits that matter—respect, responsibility, disciplined thinking, and service to others.

The public experience is built for the adults who guide young people, teens shaping their own direction, and adult men continuing the work.
