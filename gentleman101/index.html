GENTLEMAN 101™

A mentoring progression supported by books, centered on character, respect, responsibility, and growth.

Within Be Ultimate® Enterprises, GENTLEMAN 101™ reflects the founder’s commitment to help build young melanated men into capable, strong, mature gentlemen. Three mentoring tiers provide a progression, with paired book tools supporting the work of parents, mentors, and participants.
