About the enterprise

Ambition, grounded in stewardship.

Be Ultimate® Enterprises is a founder-led enterprise built around a simple conviction: strong businesses should create real value for the people they touch. We pair high standards with practical opportunity, disciplined systems with human judgment, and enterprise with responsibility.

Across our portfolio, the work is different—but the posture is consistent: build carefully, serve honestly, and create pathways that endure beyond a single transaction or program.

Founder & Principal

S. Bernard Mitchell, PhD, CET, is the founder of Be Ultimate® Enterprises. A Senior Fire Sprinkler Inspector and fire-protection subject-matter expert, he brings hands-on industry experience to technical education and workforce development through SBE Academy. He is also developing B-U Energy and Mining Solutions, a commodity-brokerage venture, and GENTLEMAN 101™, an international mentorship and publishing initiative that pairs books with three tiers of personal development. Across his work, Mitchell is focused on building practical pathways for learning, professional growth, character, and service.
